The Shark's Prey
Maresca's Side: Pretty When It Works
Chelsea are a good football team when they remember they are a good football team. Maresca has them playing something coherent, which is more than could be said for most of the last four years at that club. They press, they move, they have Palmer. Palmer is the problem. He finds space in the way that makes you wonder if the other team simply forgot he was there. City cannot forget he is there. The vulnerability is behind the press. When Chelsea commit and it does not work, the shape opens. Doku running at a backtracking full-back in that space is not a fair contest. City know this. The question is whether they are brave enough to go and get it early, or whether they spend the first half being respectful and the second half being desperate.
